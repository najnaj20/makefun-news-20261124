A clean one-page newspaper front page summing up the world news of Tuesday 24 November 2026 in 15 stories with large, legible type. Two big leads on top: the Abu Dhabi peace talks end with no deal, Russia ruling out territorial concessions while US envoys head to Moscow next; and Sanofi agrees to buy US biotech Insmed for $41 billion in cash, the year's biggest biopharma deal. Below them, thirteen more stories in two wide columns across the Middle East, the Ukraine war and the wider world, each with its source listed in STORIES.md. Every story happened or was first reported on Tuesday 24 November 2026, and the page carries a visible DATE CHECK box saying so. The image is drawn in code by render.py (Pillow + DejaVu) from verified reports (Reuters, AP, BBC, Nexus Europa, Sanofi, Kyiv Independent, WHO, UN, ESA, Just Security); main.ts copies it to the output directory.

## Run it

mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out node main.ts
